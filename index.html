What you will take away

The approach, not just the tools

We will introduce you to the tools. But the two days are mostly about the approach: the mental framework that helps you get real value out of AI, in your own job and across your business.

  1. 01

    A mental framework for AI

    A clear way to think about what AI is good at, so you can use any tool better.

  2. 02

    Cut through the noise

    There is a lot of noise around AI. Learn to understand it clearly and decide for yourself what works for you and what does not.

  3. 03

    Where to use it, and where not to

    Choose the parts of your work and business where AI helps, and recognise the places where it does not belong.

  4. 04

    Your own job, first

    Apply AI to the work you do every day and raise your own productivity, hands-on, on your own laptop.

  5. 05

    Lead the adoption

    Go back as the person in your company who uses AI first and best, and let it trickle down to your team.
