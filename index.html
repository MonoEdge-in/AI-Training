Why this workshop

The approach matters more than the tools

There is a lot of noise around AI.

New tools, big claims, and conflicting advice. We will introduce you to the tools, but the two days are mostly about the approach: a mental framework that helps you understand AI clearly and decide for yourself what works for you, what does not, and where it belongs in your business.

What you will take away

Five things you leave with

  1. A mental framework for AI

    A clear way to think about what AI is good at, so you get more out of any tool you use.

  2. A filter for the noise

    Understand what is real and choose what works for you, and what does not.

  3. Where to use it, and where not to

    Pick the parts of your work and business where AI helps, and recognise where it does not belong.

  4. Higher productivity in your own job

    Hands-on, on your own laptop, applied to the work you do every day.

  5. The confidence to lead adoption

    Go back as the person in your company who uses AI first and best. When the owner leads, it trickles down to the team.
